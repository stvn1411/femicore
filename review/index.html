FemiCore Side Effects & Safety

FemiCore uses ingredients that most people tolerate well, but a few points are worth knowing before you start.

🙂 Common, mild

Probiotics can cause a little bloating or gas in the first week while your body adjusts. This usually settles on its own.

🤰 Pregnancy & breastfeeding

Bearberry and berberine are generally not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding, so FemiCore isn't a good fit during that time.

💊 If you take medication

Berberine can interact with some medicines, including blood-sugar medication, and cranberry may interact with blood thinners. Ask your doctor first.

🩺 When to see a doctor

Pain, burning, fever, blood in the urine or symptoms that suddenly get worse need a doctor, not a supplement. FemiCore is for everyday bladder support.
